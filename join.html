Who we are

Women who've been through it — and found God in it.

We The Church is a sisterhood of women who take God at His Word. We receive Him as Savior. We serve Him. We die to our own ideas and replace them with His truth — because He is the truth, the way, and the life.

We come as we are. But we don't stay that way. If you leave exactly the same as you came, something's wrong. That's not a threat — it's the promise. The Word is alive and active, and it does not leave us unchanged.

  • We let Scripture say what it says — not what we wish it said.
  • We study in community because iron sharpens iron.
  • We come from different backgrounds and carry different stories.
  • We are not a denomination. We are not a building. We are a people.
  • We serve. We pray. We show up for each other.
