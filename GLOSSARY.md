# GLOSSARY — wethechurch

Terms and naming conventions used in this repo.

---

## What this is
We The Church — a Bible study community site for women done with church hurt and watered-down truth.
Live at: `wethechurch.pages.dev`

| Folder | What it is |

## Series naming
**Truth series** — each page addresses a specific theological topic directly from scripture.
Naming convention: `/truth/[topic-slug]/index.html` · CSS: `truth-[topic-slug].css`
